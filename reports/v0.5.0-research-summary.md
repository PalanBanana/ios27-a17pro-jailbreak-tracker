# v0.5.0 research summary: Secure Exclaves architecture and A17 Pro exclave applicability

**Status:** data as of 2026-11-12. Target: iPhone 15 Pro Max (A17 Pro, iPhone16,2), iOS 27.0.1, build 24A446. **Readiness: LEVEL 0 / NO-GO, unchanged.** This release analyses one new Apple document and reconciles firmware metadata. It does not establish compatibility with the target.

## 1. What was done

- Apple's Secure Exclaves article (published 2026-11-12) was read in full. Quoted phrases were checked by exact match against the fetched text. Architecture, terminology and map notes were written: [architecture](../research/apple-secure-exclaves-architecture.md), [terminology](../research/apple-security-terminology.md) and [map](secure-exclaves-architecture-map.md).
- Every BuildIdentity of the A17 Pro manifests (24A446, 24B119, 24B156), the A18 Pro manifest (24B119) and the A19 Pro manifest (24A446) was enumerated. Manifest hashes were preserved. See [reconciliation](../research/exclave-manifest-reconciliation.md) and [analysis/exclave-buildidentity-summary.json](../analysis/exclave-buildidentity-summary.json).
- Static exclave-identifier counts were taken from the target build's decompressed kernelcache and SPTM image. Counts only.
- The public XNU source was searched for exclave and chip identifiers. Counts, paths and board-configuration identifiers were recorded.
- Applicability was assessed field by field: [applicability](a17pro-exclave-applicability.md). Boundaries, the security matrix and the dependency notes were written.
- SKVLENT, Erosion and the jailbreak ecosystem repositories were re-checked: [SKVLENT audit](../research/skvlent-latest-update-audit.md), [SKVLENT reconciliation](skvlent-evidence-reconciliation.md), [Erosion status](../research/erosion-current-status.md), [Erosion risk](erosion-a17pro-risk-assessment.md) and [ecosystem update](../research/jailbreak-ecosystem-v050-update.md).

## 2. What was not done, by design

- No device was contacted, connected, updated, restored, rebooted or used. No firmware was installed.
- Erosion was not installed, its IPA was not downloaded or built, no signing material was generated, and worse_query was not run.
- No probe, exploit, Bench27 build or run was made, and no exploit-development folder was reviewed.
- No code-level or binary-level differencing was performed, and no binary was mapped to an advisory. Both remain declined.
- No further extraction was made from firmware packages. The XNU source was searched, not read in full.

## 3. Main results

1. Apple's article names five products (iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone Duo, MacBook Neo and HomeView) and no A-series, M-series or T-series chip. It names no iPhone 15 Pro Max or A17 Pro (C-139).
2. Apple's architecture statements are recorded as Apple statements (C-140 to C-151, C-168, C-169). The article does not state how any of them is implemented on a specific device.
3. None of the nine A17 Pro BuildIdentities for builds 24A446, 24B119 and 24B156 lists an exclave-named component (C-157). The A18 Pro and A19 Pro manifests list six in each erase and update identity (C-158).
4. The A17 Pro manifest chip identifier (0x8130) differs from the monitor token (t8122). For the A18 Pro and A19 Pro the token and identifier agree (C-159). The XNU source groups T8122 with T8130 (C-154). A community wiki gives T8130 for the A17 Pro (C-160, partially verified).
5. The target build's kernelcache contains 702 exclave-prefixed identifier occurrences (187 distinct forms). Its SPTM image contains 13 (10 distinct). These are static strings only (C-161). The SPTM image also contains strings naming the exclaveOS integrity catalog and trust cache (C-163).
6. Applicability of Secure Exclaves to the A17 Pro is not established (C-162).
7. Repository statements re-checked: the SKVLENT head is unchanged (C-164). Direction 10 is described as a container-boundary crossing in userland, not kernel code execution (C-165). Erosion's release notes and README disagree on iOS 26 tweaks (C-166). Ecosystem heads and releases are unchanged (C-167).

## 4. Corrections and superseded statements

- The earlier A17 Pro record used the component list of identity 0 only. The complete enumeration supersedes it for exclave purposes (C-157, C-158). The earlier claims C-119 to C-125 remain true.
- C-122 and C-123 are backed by the complete enumeration. C-124 (the chip-token mapping) remains unverified.
- A lookup against opa334/ElleKit was a wrong target. The former ElleKit repository is tealbathingsuit/ellekit (C-167).
- A search summary said that earlier chips lack exclaves. The 2025 press article does not say this, and the statement is not recorded as a claim.

## 5. Contradictions recorded and not resolved

1. Apple's article gives two accounts of the microkernel's origin: an Apple-customised version of the L4 microkernel, and development from scratch at Apple (C-141).
2. The A17 Pro monitor token (t8122) and the manifest chip identifier (0x8130) differ (C-159).
3. SKVLENT's second-device build is 24B115 in one document and 24B119 in others (C-116). Its direction 03 result on 27.2 is recorded against Apple's fixed-in-27.2 listing (C-110, C-047).
4. Erosion's header states 26.7.2 and its supported list states 26.6.2 (C-126, C-127). Its release notes and FAQ differ on iOS 26 tweaks (C-166).
5. The planning brief lists ARM64 and arm64e. The repository's device profile records arm64 without a source. The classes are not resolved.
6. Press reports of exclave references tied to M4 and A18 processors are unverified (C-155, C-156).
